NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE CODE OF CONDUCT

Document ID: HR-POL-001
Department: Human Resources
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE

Northstar Technologies is committed to maintaining a professional, ethical, respectful, and inclusive working environment. This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of behavior expected from all employees, contractors, and temporary staff.

Every team member is responsible for understanding and following these standards in their daily work.

2. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR

Employees must act honestly, responsibly, and professionally when interacting with colleagues, customers, suppliers, and business partners.

Employees are expected to:
- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Complete assigned responsibilities with reasonable care and diligence.
- Respect colleagues' time, contributions, and professional boundaries.
- Cooperate with team members to achieve shared objectives.
- Report misconduct or unethical behavior through the appropriate channels.

Employees must not engage in harassment, intimidation, discrimination, bullying, or deliberate actions intended to undermine another person's work.

3. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY AND INCLUSION

Northstar Technologies promotes equal opportunity and a workplace where individuals are treated with dignity and respect.

Employment decisions must be based on relevant qualifications, skills, performance, and business requirements.

Discrimination or harassment based on legally protected characteristics is prohibited. Employees who experience or witness inappropriate conduct should report the situation to Human Resources or through the designated reporting channel.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, subject to the need to investigate and comply with applicable law.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation is prohibited.

4. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

Employees must avoid situations in which personal interests interfere, or appear to interfere, with their professional responsibilities or Northstar Technologies' legitimate business interests.

Examples of potential conflicts include:
- Participating in purchasing decisions involving a family member's business.
- Accepting gifts that could improperly influence a business decision.
- Using confidential company information for personal financial gain.
- Maintaining an outside business activity that directly conflicts with assigned responsibilities.

Employees must disclose potential conflicts to their manager or Human Resources as soon as reasonably possible. Disclosed conflicts will be reviewed individually, and appropriate safeguards may be required.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

Employees must protect confidential business information, employee records, customer information, financial data, intellectual property, and other sensitive information accessed through their work.

Employees must:
- Access information only when authorized and required for their responsibilities.
- Share confidential information only with authorized recipients.
- Use approved systems and channels to store and transmit company information.
- Protect passwords, authentication credentials, and access tokens.
- Report suspected data breaches or unauthorized access promptly.

Confidential information must not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals, including after employment ends, unless disclosure is legally authorized or required.

6. USE OF COMPANY ASSETS

Company equipment, software, accounts, networks, and other resources must be used responsibly and primarily for legitimate business purposes.

Employees must not:
- Install unauthorized software on company devices.
- Share company credentials with other individuals.
- Use company systems to conduct illegal activities.
- Deliberately damage, disable, or misuse company assets.
- Copy or distribute company-owned intellectual property without authorization.

Limited personal use may be permitted when consistent with company policies and when it does not interfere with work, compromise security, or create additional costs.

7. COMMUNICATION AND COLLABORATION

Employees are expected to communicate clearly, respectfully, and professionally across teams and departments.

Workplace disagreements should be addressed constructively. Employees should focus on evidence, business objectives, and practical solutions rather than personal attacks.

Employees must not knowingly distribute false information about colleagues, customers, or the company.

Company communications, including emails, internal messages, reports, and public statements made on behalf of Northstar Technologies, must be accurate and appropriate for their intended audience.

Only authorized representatives may issue official statements on behalf of the company.

8. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS AND POLICIES

Employees must comply with applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, and internal company policies relevant to their responsibilities.

Employees must not falsify records, manipulate financial information, offer improper payments, or knowingly participate in fraudulent activities.

Questions about legal, regulatory, or ethical obligations should be directed to the employee's manager, Human Resources, or the appropriate compliance contact.

Employees who become aware of suspected violations should report them through an appropriate internal channel.

9. HEALTH, SAFETY, AND WELL-BEING

Northstar Technologies seeks to maintain a safe and healthy working environment for employees working on company premises, at customer locations, or remotely.

Employees must follow applicable health and safety procedures, report workplace hazards, and avoid actions that create unreasonable risks to themselves or others.

Threats, workplace violence, and working while impaired in a manner that creates a safety risk are prohibited.

Employees should seek assistance when workplace conditions or responsibilities create significant safety concerns.

10. USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS

Employees may use artificial intelligence tools when their use is authorized and consistent with company policies.

Employees must:
- Use approved AI tools for company work.
- Avoid entering confidential, personal, or sensitive company information into unapproved AI systems.
- Review AI-generated content for accuracy, relevance, and potential bias.
- Verify important factual claims before relying on AI-generated outputs.
- Respect intellectual property rights and applicable data protection requirements.
- Remain accountable for decisions and deliverables produced with AI assistance.

AI-generated content must not be treated as automatically accurate or approved for external distribution.

11. REPORTING CONCERNS

Employees who observe or experience conduct that may violate this Code should report the concern to their manager, Human Resources, or the designated reporting channel.

Reports should include relevant facts, dates, individuals involved, and supporting information when available.

Employees are expected to cooperate honestly with authorized investigations.

Knowingly submitting false allegations or deliberately obstructing an investigation may result in disciplinary action.

Northstar Technologies prohibits retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith.

12. VIOLATIONS AND DISCIPLINARY ACTION

Violations of this Code may result in corrective or disciplinary action, depending on the nature, severity, frequency, and circumstances of the conduct.

Possible actions include:
- Coaching or additional training.
- Formal written warnings.
- Restriction of access to company systems or resources.
- Suspension or termination of employment, where appropriate and legally permitted.
- Referral to relevant authorities when required by law.

Disciplinary decisions will be handled in accordance with applicable law, employment agreements, and relevant company procedures.

13. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES

Every employee is responsible for:
- Reading and understanding this Code.
- Following applicable company policies.
- Seeking clarification when requirements are unclear.
- Reporting suspected violations through appropriate channels.
- Completing any required ethics or compliance training.

Managers are additionally responsible for promoting ethical conduct, addressing concerns appropriately, and helping employees understand their obligations.

14. POLICY REVIEW

Human Resources is responsible for coordinating periodic reviews of this Code in consultation with relevant departments.

Northstar Technologies may revise this document when business requirements, operational practices, or applicable legal obligations change.

Employees will be informed of material changes through the company's established communication channels.

15. ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Employees may be required to acknowledge that they have received, read, and understood this Code of Conduct.

Acknowledgment does not replace the employee's obligation to comply with applicable laws, contractual requirements, and other company policies.
