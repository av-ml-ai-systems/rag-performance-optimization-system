# Northstar Technologies — Business Travel Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-POL-003  
**Department:** Finance and Operations  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the requirements for planning, approving, booking, and reimbursing business travel at Northstar Technologies. Its purpose is to ensure that travel expenses are reasonable, necessary, properly documented, and aligned with company objectives.

This policy applies to employees and other authorized individuals traveling on behalf of Northstar Technologies.

## 2. Travel Authorization

All business travel must receive approval from the employee's manager before bookings are made or expenses are incurred.

A travel request should include:

- Business purpose and expected outcomes.
- Destination and proposed travel dates.
- Estimated transportation and accommodation costs.
- Names of employees traveling, when applicable.
- Relevant customer meetings, conferences, or business events.

Travel involving international destinations, unusually high costs, or significant schedule changes may require additional approval from the relevant department director.

Employees must not assume that a travel request has been approved until they receive explicit confirmation.

## 3. Transportation Bookings

Employees should select transportation options that balance cost, safety, travel time, and business requirements.

Economy-class airfare is the standard for flights unless an exception is approved by the employee's department director.

Employees should book travel as early as reasonably possible to obtain competitive prices. When practical, they should compare available routes, schedules, and total costs before selecting an option.

Personal travel extensions must be separated from business expenses. Any additional costs resulting from personal travel are the employee's responsibility unless explicitly approved under an applicable company policy.

Employees must obtain approval before purchasing unusually expensive tickets or making nonrefundable bookings when the travel arrangements remain uncertain.

## 4. Accommodation

Hotel accommodations must be appropriate for the destination, duration, and purpose of the trip.

Employees should select reasonably priced accommodation that meets safety, accessibility, and business requirements.

When available and suitable, employees should consider corporate hotel rates or accommodations recommended by the company.

Accommodation upgrades, premium amenities, and expenses associated with accompanying family members are not reimbursable unless specifically authorized in advance.

Employees sharing accommodation must ensure that the arrangement complies with applicable company requirements and respects individual privacy.

## 5. Meals and Daily Expenses

Reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel may be reimbursed in accordance with the company's expense reimbursement procedures.

Employees must follow any applicable daily spending limits communicated by Finance.

Meal expenses should be proportionate to the destination and business circumstances. Lavish meals and personal entertainment are not reimbursable unless an authorized exception applies.

When meals are provided by a conference, customer, hotel, or other organization, employees should not claim duplicate reimbursement for those meals.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable unless explicitly authorized under a separate applicable policy.

## 6. Ground Transportation

Reasonable ground transportation expenses may be reimbursed when necessary for approved business activities.

Permitted expenses may include:

- Public transportation.
- Standard taxi services.
- Approved ride-hailing services.
- Airport transfers.
- Rental vehicles when justified by business requirements.
- Parking and tolls associated with authorized travel.

Employees should use safe and cost-effective transportation options whenever practical.

Rental vehicles require prior approval when their estimated cost exceeds the department's normal travel budget or when a suitable alternative is available at substantially lower cost.

Traffic fines, personal transportation unrelated to business activities, and unauthorized vehicle upgrades are not reimbursable.

## 7. Expense Documentation

Employees must retain itemized receipts and supporting documentation for expenses requiring reimbursement.

Each expense submission should identify:

- The date of the expense.
- The amount and currency.
- The expense category.
- The business purpose.
- The associated trip or project.
- The receipt or other required supporting evidence.

If a receipt is unavailable, the employee must follow the company's documented exception procedure and provide a reasonable explanation.

Employees must not submit duplicate claims, alter receipts, misrepresent personal expenses as business expenses, or claim expenses paid directly by the company.

## 8. Expense Submission and Reimbursement

Employees must submit travel expense claims through the company's approved expense management process.

Claims should be submitted within 15 calendar days after the completion of the business trip, unless Finance communicates a different deadline.

Managers are responsible for reviewing the business purpose and reasonableness of submitted expenses. Finance is responsible for verifying documentation, checking compliance with this policy, and processing eligible reimbursements.

Incomplete claims may be returned to the employee for correction. Reimbursement may be delayed until the required documentation and approvals are provided.

Approved reimbursements are normally processed within 10 business days after Finance receives a complete and approved claim. Processing times may vary when additional review is required.

## 9. Cancellations and Travel Changes

Employees must notify their manager and the appropriate travel coordinator as soon as they become aware that an approved trip must be changed or canceled.

When practical, employees should select refundable or changeable bookings when the additional cost is reasonable in relation to the risk of cancellation.

Employees must not incur avoidable cancellation fees or change charges without a valid business reason.

If a trip is canceled, employees must make reasonable efforts to obtain refunds or travel credits and document any amounts that cannot be recovered.

Changes required by emergencies, significant operational needs, or circumstances outside the employee's control should be documented and reviewed according to the applicable approval process.

## 10. International Travel

International business travel requires approval before bookings are made.

Employees traveling internationally are responsible for confirming that they have the required travel documents, visas, vaccinations where applicable, and other necessary authorizations.

The company may provide additional guidance regarding travel insurance, destination-specific security concerns, data protection, and emergency contacts.

Employees must comply with applicable laws and company policies in the countries they visit.

Company devices and confidential information must be protected during international travel. Employees should consult IT or the security team before traveling to destinations where additional security measures may be required.

## 11. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require documented approval from the appropriate manager or department director before the expense is incurred whenever reasonably possible.

Requests for exceptions should explain the business justification, estimated cost, and reason the standard policy cannot be followed.

Emergency circumstances may prevent advance approval. In such cases, employees must notify their manager as soon as practical and provide supporting documentation afterward.

Finance may request additional information before approving an exception or processing a reimbursement.

## 12. Responsibilities

**Employees** are responsible for obtaining travel approval, choosing reasonable travel options, retaining receipts, submitting accurate expense claims, and complying with this policy.

**Managers** are responsible for reviewing travel requests, confirming business necessity, approving appropriate expenses, and ensuring that travel arrangements align with departmental budgets.

**Finance** is responsible for maintaining expense procedures, reviewing reimbursement claims, monitoring policy compliance, and communicating applicable reimbursement requirements.

**Human Resources and Operations** may assist with employee travel requirements, safety procedures, and coordination when needed.

## 13. Policy Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in reimbursement denial, a request to repay improperly reimbursed expenses, or disciplinary action in accordance with company procedures and applicable law.

Suspected expense fraud or deliberate misrepresentation may be referred to the appropriate internal review function.

Employees who are uncertain whether an expense is permitted should seek clarification from their manager or Finance before incurring it.

## 14. Review and Updates

Finance and Operations are responsible for reviewing this policy periodically and coordinating updates when business requirements, travel practices, or applicable legal obligations change.

Employees should consult the current approved version before arranging business travel.
