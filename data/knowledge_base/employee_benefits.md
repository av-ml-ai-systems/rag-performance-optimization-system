# Northstar Technologies — Employee Benefits Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-004  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the employee benefits available at Northstar Technologies, the general eligibility requirements, and the procedures for accessing and maintaining those benefits.

Benefits are designed to support employee well-being, professional development, and work-life balance. Specific benefits may vary according to employment status, applicable law, location, and the terms of individual benefit plans.

## 2. Eligibility

Regular full-time employees are generally eligible for the standard employee benefits described in this policy, subject to the requirements of each benefit program.

Part-time employees, temporary workers, contractors, and other nonstandard employment categories may have different eligibility conditions.

Eligibility begins on the employee's official start date unless a particular benefit specifies a different waiting period or enrollment date.

Human Resources determines eligibility based on employment records and the requirements of each applicable program.

Employees should contact Human Resources if they believe their eligibility information is incorrect.

## 3. Health and Wellness Benefits

Northstar Technologies supports employee health through applicable health coverage, wellness resources, and programs designed to encourage healthy working practices.

Available benefits may include health insurance, access to wellness resources, and information about preventive care.

Coverage details, participating providers, employee contributions, exclusions, and enrollment deadlines are defined by the applicable benefit plan.

Employees should review the official plan documents before making healthcare or coverage decisions.

The company may periodically update wellness offerings based on employee needs, available providers, and organizational resources.

## 4. Paid Time Off

Eligible employees may receive paid time off for vacation, personal needs, and other purposes established by the company's leave policies.

Vacation requests must be submitted through the approved leave management process and approved by the employee's manager before the planned absence, except when circumstances make advance approval impractical.

Managers should consider business requirements while ensuring that employees have reasonable opportunities to use their eligible leave.

Unused leave, carryover limits, accrual rates, and payment of unused balances are governed by the applicable leave policy and local legal requirements.

This policy does not replace the separate Employee Leave Policy.

## 5. Parental and Family Support

Eligible employees may access parental leave and other family-related benefits in accordance with applicable company policies and legal requirements.

Employees should contact Human Resources as early as practical to understand eligibility, required documentation, and the process for requesting leave.

Requests involving sensitive personal information must be handled confidentially and shared only with individuals who need the information to administer the benefit.

The company will administer applicable family-related benefits in accordance with the relevant policies and legal obligations.

## 6. Retirement and Financial Benefits

Where available, eligible employees may participate in retirement savings programs, pension arrangements, or other financial benefit plans.

Participation requirements, contribution rules, vesting conditions, and withdrawal procedures are determined by the applicable plan documents and legal requirements.

Employees are responsible for reviewing the terms of their plans and keeping their enrollment information current.

Human Resources can direct employees to the appropriate plan administrator but does not provide individualized financial or investment advice.

## 7. Learning and Professional Development

Northstar Technologies encourages employees to develop skills relevant to their current responsibilities and future career opportunities.

Depending on eligibility, budget, and business needs, professional development support may include:

- Approved technical or professional courses.
- Industry conferences and workshops.
- Certification programs.
- Internal training sessions.
- Job-related learning resources.

Employees must obtain manager approval before committing company funds to training or certification expenses.

Requests should explain the learning objective, expected cost, relevance to the employee's role, and anticipated business benefit.

Reimbursement may require proof of attendance, successful completion, or other documentation specified in the approval.

Unapproved training expenses are not automatically eligible for reimbursement.

## 8. Employee Assistance and Well-Being

Employees may have access to confidential support resources intended to assist with personal challenges, workplace concerns, or general well-being.

The availability and scope of these services depend on the programs offered in the employee's location.

Employees can contact Human Resources for information about current resources and access procedures.

Information shared through confidential assistance services should be handled according to the provider's privacy requirements and applicable law.

These services do not replace emergency assistance when immediate medical or safety intervention is required.

## 9. Remote Work and Work-Life Balance

Eligible employees may participate in remote or hybrid work arrangements when authorized under their role and the company's Remote Work Policy.

Flexible work arrangements do not automatically change eligibility for other benefits.

Employees must continue to meet their role's performance expectations, attend required meetings, protect company information, and comply with applicable working-time requirements.

Specific arrangements must be agreed upon with the employee's manager and may be reviewed when business needs change.

## 10. Enrollment and Changes

Employees must complete required enrollment forms and provide accurate information within the deadlines established for each benefit.

Certain benefits may require employees to make an active selection rather than relying on automatic enrollment.

Employees should notify Human Resources promptly when a qualifying life event or change in employment status may affect benefit eligibility or coverage.

Examples may include a change in dependent status, a change in employment category, or a change in work location that affects applicable benefit arrangements.

Supporting documentation may be required to process changes.

Late enrollment or incomplete documentation may delay access to a benefit or affect available enrollment options, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 11. Changes in Employment Status

Benefit eligibility may change when an employee changes employment category, takes an extended leave, transfers to another location, or leaves the company.

Human Resources will communicate applicable benefit continuation, modification, or termination requirements according to the relevant plan documents and legal obligations.

Employees leaving the company should contact Human Resources to understand any required actions, outstanding claims, or applicable deadlines.

The terms of each benefit plan determine how accrued balances, contributions, and coverage are handled when employment ends.

## 12. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Reviewing applicable benefit descriptions and plan documents.
- Providing accurate enrollment and dependent information.
- Meeting enrollment and documentation deadlines.
- Reporting relevant changes in eligibility.
- Using company-sponsored benefits only for authorized purposes.
- Protecting confidential information associated with benefit administration.
- Asking Human Resources for clarification when policy requirements are unclear.

Providing false information or intentionally misusing a benefit may result in corrective or disciplinary action in accordance with company procedures and applicable law.

## 13. Human Resources Responsibilities

Human Resources is responsible for communicating benefit information, maintaining appropriate employee records, coordinating enrollment, and directing employees to relevant plan administrators.

Human Resources also coordinates policy reviews and communicates material changes to benefit programs.

Benefit information must be handled confidentially and accessed only for legitimate administrative purposes.

Where a plan is administered by an external provider, the provider's official documentation governs the detailed terms of coverage, subject to applicable law.

## 14. Policy Limitations

This document provides a general overview of employee benefits. It does not guarantee that every benefit is available to every employee.

Specific benefit plans, employment agreements, company policies, and applicable legal requirements take precedence when they establish more detailed eligibility conditions or procedures.

Northstar Technologies may modify discretionary benefit offerings as business needs change, subject to contractual commitments and applicable legal requirements.

## 15. Review and Updates

Human Resources is responsible for reviewing this policy periodically and updating it when benefit programs, organizational requirements, or applicable legal obligations change.

Employees should consult the current approved version and contact Human Resources when they need clarification about a specific benefit.
